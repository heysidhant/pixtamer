Turn a folder of images into web-ready files

Pixtamer converts, compresses, resizes and renames whole batches on your Mac. It's free and open source, and nothing is uploaded.

Download for Mac

Version 0.1.0, 2.8 MB. Needs macOS 14 or later.

Four jobs, one button

Set the recipe once, or pick a saved preset, and Pixtamer runs it on every file in the batch. Here's what happened to one iPhone photo in that export.

Safe to run on real work

Pixtamer is sandboxed by macOS. It reads only the files you add and writes only to the folder you choose.

Your originals stay as they are

Every export is a new file. Pixtamer never edits the source image.

Nothing leaves your Mac

There's no account, no analytics and no upload. The app has no network access.

No surprise overwrites

Existing files stay unless you choose to replace them.

One bad file won't stop the batch

The rest keep going, and you can retry the ones that failed.

Real sizes, not estimates

The numbers come from the files Pixtamer actually wrote.

Check before you publish

Compare any image before and after, at fit or at 100%.

Formats

Reads JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, GIF, BMP and JPEG XL, as far as your version of macOS supports them.

Export asGood to know
JPEGTransparency is flattened onto white.
PNGLossless, so photos can end up larger.
WebPLossy or lossless. Metadata is removed and colours convert to sRGB.
AVIFAppears when your Mac's encoder passes a test at launch.
  • Animated GIFs export their first frame.
  • 16-bit and HDR images export as 8-bit.
  • RAW and PSD files aren't accepted.

Install Pixtamer

Open the DMG and drag Pixtamer to Applications. Builds are signed and notarized by Apple, and run on Apple silicon and Intel.

Download for Mac

Or use Homebrew

brew tap heysidhant/tap
brew trust heysidhant/tap
brew install --cask pixtamer

Prefer to build it yourself? The source is on GitHub under the MIT licence.
